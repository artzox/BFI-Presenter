BFI Presenter Log Analyzer

Drop the timing logs from a session (BFIPresenter_log_…csv, _reflex_…csv, _game_…csv) together with ReShade.log. Each setting you tried becomes a row: how evenly new frames hit the flashes, the latency, and what to try next. Files are read in this browser only and never uploaded.

Drop log files here Several sessions at once are fine: they are grouped by their date and time stamp and compared at the end.
How it reads the logs: a flash is a lit refresh. At the cap in use the presenter expects a set number of new game frames per flash (one at Half, two at Refresh with 2 frames per cycle). Repeated counts flashes that showed the previous frame again, skipped counts flashes where a frame was dropped. Seconds below 3 fps (loading) and hitch-guard pauses are left out. Input to flash is the game's Reflex time from input to present plus the presenter's release-to-flash time, so it needs a Reflex game. Rows under 8 seconds are shown but not ranked.
